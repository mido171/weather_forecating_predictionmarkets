2. **Broad feature soup is unreliable.** Family separation and OOF stacking are mandatory.
3. **Hard sparse cells do not transfer.** Replace them with shrinkage, smooth probabilities and parent fallback.
4. **Additional no-harm gates often do nothing.** 0195, 0197, 0198 and 0203 effectively preserve the parent.
5. **Mature T-7 synoptic trajectories did not improve 0196.** Do not widen the same lagged feature matrix without a different response or source.

7. **Source-era routing is not a free edge.** Additional expert routing must beat fixed blends and simple memory.
8. **Short-history high-frequency scores are not strict T-24 evidence.** Keep them prospective or as teacher signals.
9. **0.799/0.874 roadmap scores are invalid.** They use same-row target/error-derived fields and duplicated predictions.
10. **Unproven source timing cannot be cured by model validation.** A strong retrospective relationship remains diagnostic until availability is proved.
