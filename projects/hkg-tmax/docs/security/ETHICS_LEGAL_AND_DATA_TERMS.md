# Ethics, Legal, and Data Terms

## Lawful research only

Use public, licensed, or properly purchased data. Respect:

- provider terms;
- attribution;
- rate limits;
- access controls;
- redistribution constraints;
- market platform rules;
- applicable law and tax/regulatory obligations.

## Absolute prohibition

Never:

- tamper with a weather station or its environment;
- attempt to influence a reading;
- access restricted provider systems;
- obtain confidential publication information;
- manipulate settlement;
- coordinate deceptive market behavior;
- evade technical controls.

The competitive advantage must come from lawful forecasting, data engineering, and execution quality.

## Data licensing ledger

Every source contract records:

- owner/provider;
- source URL;
- access date;
- license/terms URL;
- allowed storage/use;
- allowed redistribution;
- attribution requirement;
- credentials;
- review date.

Third-party raw data should normally remain excluded from the ZIP/Git unless redistribution is expressly allowed.

## Market risk

Prediction-market participation can lose the full amount risked. Model uncertainty, liquidity, fees, and source changes matter. The system must never represent a forecast as certainty or profitability as guaranteed.

## Privacy/security

Most project data is nonpersonal meteorological/market data. Nevertheless:

- do not commit credentials;
- redact authenticated headers;
- restrict trading keys;
- separate research and execution permissions;
- preserve audit logs.
