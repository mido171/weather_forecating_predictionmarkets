# Outside Knowledge Policy

## Allowed use

The Director may consult authoritative:

- meteorological science;
- HKO/NOAA source documentation;
- forecast verification literature;
- boundary-layer and coastal meteorology;
- statistical/time-series/spatial methods;
- operational NWP documentation;
- market-settlement documentation later.

Use outside knowledge to generate mechanisms, feature formulas, validation methods, or exact source contracts.

## Required provenance

For every externally inspired experiment, record:

- source title/organization;
- access date;
- citation or local archived note;
- concept imported;
- mapping to repository variables;
- assumptions;
- local falsification test.

Do not copy a claim into the system without local validation.

## Data acquisition gate

Before proposing a new external dataset, specify:

- exact source;
- legal/license status;
- variables;
- spatial domain;
- cadence;
- issue/release time;
- valid time;
- historical vintage availability;
- lead/cycle;
- byte budget;
- storage;
- parser;
- point-in-time proof;
- specific unresolved signal gap.

Broad “download NWP” proposals are rejected.

## Temporal truth

Current documentation can establish a general publication process only when it applies to historical rows or a prospective live system. It cannot retroactively prove all archive rows had the same latency without evidence.

## Separation

External knowledge may guide diagnostic research. It may not override the repository's cutoff contract, unlock a blocked source by assumption, or justify target-day observations.
